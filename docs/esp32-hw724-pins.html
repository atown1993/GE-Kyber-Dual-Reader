HiLetgo HW-724 ESP32 — what plugs into each header pin

Hold the board with the antenna at the top, OLED + USB at the bottom, component side up. Pinout verified against a physical HW-724 (2026-09-03); it is identical to the Wemos LOLIN32-OLED layout. Rows are numbered from the top on each side.

Why the pin plan changed (vs. the first breadboard map)

  • This board only brings out 13 usable GPIOs. The left column's SD1 / CMD / SD0 / CLK are the flash chip's pins — touching them crashes the ESP32. GPIO 32/33/23/19/18/17/27 from the earlier map don't exist on this board.
  • SVP (GPIO36) and SVN (GPIO39) are input-only — perfect for the two reader RX lines, useless for anything else. That frees 25 and 26 for LED duty.
  • GPIO0 and GPIO12 are boot pins. A MOSFET gate is a safe load (it pulls nothing). An LED is NOT: it drags the pin low at reset — GPIO0 low = the board boots into download mode; GPIO12 high = wrong flash voltage. So gates live on 0/12, LEDs on 25/26/2/13/14/15.
  • GPIO15 driven low at boot only silences the ROM boot log. GPIO2 low at boot is fine. Harmless side effects, noted so they don't surprise you.
  • Don't press BOOT while the sketch is running (it shorts GPIO0, which is now an output). During upload it's fine — the chip is in reset.
